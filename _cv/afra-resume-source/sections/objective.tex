% Objective Statement

I focus on post-training, evaluation, and continual learning for large language models, with an emphasis on real-world, high-stakes reasoning. My recent work spans online reinforcement learning (OnlineRubrics, RL4F), agentic systems, human data programs for post-training and evaluation, and long-horizon workflows involving file processing and artifact generation. I led the development of PRBench and DrugDiscoveryBench, large-scale evaluations of language models and agents across professional domains including finance, legal services, healthcare, and life sciences.
% I want AI to have a reliable understanding of both the \textbf{world} by improving factuality \& coherence and the individual \textbf{users} via personalization. I produced novel \textbf{algorithms for synthetic data generation} that improve language models' \textbf{factual correctness} and enable \textbf{consistent knowledge updates} when new information is available. Additionally, I introduced a new benchmark for \textbf{model editing} driven by language instructions to improve factuality as well as personalization. Moreover, I developed robust approaches for detecting and mitigating \textbf{social biases in LLMs} to support alignment with user preferences. Finally, I created a learned \textbf{feedback generator with RL} as a proxy user to improve LM's answers after deployment.

% I work on creating language models that have globally coherent and manipulable world models. My research achieves this by combining synthetic data generation with symbolic approaches and training external models that generate synthetic feedback in natural language. My other focus is on creating models that learn from individual users' preferences expressed in natural language and have accurate models of users \textit{i.e. personalization}.
